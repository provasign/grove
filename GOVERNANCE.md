# Governance

Grove is maintainer-led and evidence-governed. Contributors propose and test
changes; reviewers approve areas where they have sustained expertise;
maintainers merge, release, handle security reports, and define compatibility
and quality tiers.

Public API, graph semantics, quality tiers, storage migrations, licensing, and
governance changes require a written design issue and maintainer consensus.
Benchmark claims require reproducible truth data, corpus pins, and disclosed
limitations. Maintainer status is granted by consensus after sustained,
trustworthy contribution.
